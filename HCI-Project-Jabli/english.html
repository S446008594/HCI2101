Ethics in Human-Computer Interaction

Course Code: HCI 2204

A. General Information

Institution: Umm Al Qura University

College: Computing

Department: Software Engineering

Level/Year: 2nd Year / 4th Level

Credit Hours: 2 Hours

Description:

This course examines ethical considerations in human-computer interaction, focusing on moral principles, privacy, intellectual property, and social responsibilities.

B. Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

Code Learning Outcomes Teaching Strategies Assessment Methods
1.1 Explain ethical, legal, and social frameworks relevant to HCI. Discussions, Case studies Assignments / Quizzes / Exams
2.1 Communicate ethical analyses effectively through case studies. Discussions, Demonstrations Research Project
3.1 Demonstrate informed judgment and professional ethical principles. Case studies, Discussions Research project / Exams

C. Course Content

  1. Introduction to Ethics in HCI
  2. Ethical Frameworks and Theories
  3. Privacy, Security, and Civil Liberties
  4. Intellectual Property and Software Ethics
  5. Social Implications of Computing
  6. Inclusivity, Accessibility, and Cultural Contexts
  7. Professional Responsibility in HCI
  8. Emerging Technologies and Ethical Challenges
  9. Case Studies in HCI Ethics
  10. Ethical Research and Experimentation

D. Lessons Learned

  • Deep understanding of protecting user privacy and data security during interaction systems design.
  • Ability to differentiate between intellectual property rights and apply computing professional codes of ethics.
  • Recognizing the importance of inclusive design and ensuring accessibility for all users, including people with disabilities.
  • Formulating informed and responsible engineering choices aligned with global and local legal frameworks.
