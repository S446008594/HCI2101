Ethics in Human-Computer Interaction

Course Code: HCI 2204

A. General Information

Institution: Umm Al Qura University

College: Computer Science and Engineering

Department: Software Engineering

Level/Year: 2nd Year / 4th Level

Credit Hours: 2 Hours

Description:

This course examines ethical considerations in human-computer interaction, focusing on moral principles, privacy, intellectual property, and social responsibilities.

B. Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

Code Learning Outcomes Assessment Methods
1.1 Explain ethical, legal, and social frameworks. Quizzes / Exams
2.1 Communicate ethical analyses effectively. Research Project
3.1 Demonstrate professional ethical principles. Exams / Research Project

C. Course Content

  1. Introduction to Ethics in HCI
  2. Ethical Frameworks and Theories
  3. Privacy, Security, and Civil Liberties
  4. Intellectual Property and Software Ethics
  5. Social Implications of Computing
  6. Inclusivity and Accessibility
